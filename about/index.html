Make useful AI something people can build with, understand and improve.

DAO Studio connects product development, consulting and research around that purpose. Its founding ambition was to help people create decentralised, autonomous organisations, inspired by Amul and Lijjat Papad. Shared capability could give a smaller organisation more to build with, while leaving people more time for meaningful work and relationships.

The ambition was non-zero-sum: helping one business grow should make more possible for the others around it. The studio would help build and maintain that shared network as its guardian, not its owner. Its early focus was small and medium businesses and women in tier-2 and tier-3 India, with simpler access to useful services and more control over their time and data.

As the work moved into AI, that ambition became a mission to democratise AI and commoditise intelligence. A useful capability should fit the work people actually do, let them contribute what they know and become easier for another team to adopt. YOJN became the name around which the studio explored explainability, controllability, decomposability and interoperability: understanding an answer, guiding it, working with its parts and connecting it to other systems.

Three ways to make capability useful

From detection to response

YOJN Care connects the person asking for help, the monitored site and the response team around one incident. Personal SOS, camera evidence and human review help the next person understand what happened and carry the response onward. It is a product for response teams, not an emergency service; if you need help now, call your local emergency number.

YOJN Care

The application around the model

YojnAI connects models, tools, agents and checked playbooks to an organisation’s records and services. People can supply missing context, inspect the work behind an answer and use feedback to decide what to improve.

YojnAI

Sharing useful capability

DAOnet is a protocol design for letting a useful capability travel beyond its first application; no marketplace runs. Receiving teams can find it, configure it for their work and keep its context across requests. Contributors and receiving teams share an account of the exchange, so both can follow what was used and what happened.

DAOnet

A service can solve one team’s problem. A product should let the useful part accumulate.

Consulting starts with an organisation’s records, rules and continuing work. Turning service work into reusable capability means finding the mechanism another team can adopt while each customer keeps their own context. A product then carries the learning forward, making the next adoption easier.

An accessible AI service needs the organisation behind the conversation.

A natural conversation can make a service easier to reach. Behind it, people still need to supply business knowledge, inspect an unexpected result and correct the operation. Care, YojnAI and DAOnet address distinct parts of that work: human response, composed applications and capability shared between teams.

People, products and a route into your work.

Harsh Joshi and Shashwat Pandey founded DAO Studio in October 2022. Care grew from Harsh’s earlier work leading product and engineering at Sachait, an emergency-response company; YojnAI reached pilots in investment banking, operations and the CFO office. Those experiences connect the studio’s product questions to Harsh’s wider work on making complex systems useful to people.
