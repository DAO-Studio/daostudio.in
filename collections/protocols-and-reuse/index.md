Title: Protocols and reuse

[Read HTML](https://daostudio.in/collections/protocols-and-reuse/)

Author: Harsh Joshi

Useful work should be able to accumulate. A team that has made an invoice-reading capability reliable ought to be able to contribute something to the next application without rebuilding the entire service. The difficulty is deciding what that contribution actually contains. A package of code, a customer's business rules, a trained model and an operating service ask different things of the team receiving them.

An interface tells a consumer how to invoke [a reusable capability](https://daostudio.in/collections/protocols-and-reuse/some-things-never-change/); its version, dependencies and evaluation help explain when the result can be useful. The conditions of use determine whether it can travel at all. [Service work can reveal the repeated need](https://daostudio.in/blog/turning-service-work-into-reusable-capability/), while still leaving a particular customer's data and commitments with them. The next adoption becomes easier through a stable contribution, not through quietly carrying those private dependencies forward.

A network adds both a coordination problem and an institutional one. Contributors and consumers may need a common account of which version was supplied, which terms were accepted and what happened to a transaction. They also need a way to [challenge a decision or change a rule](https://daostudio.in/collections/protocols-and-reuse/a-court-before-the-consequence/a-precognition-court-for-ai/). [A shared ledger](https://daostudio.in/collections/protocols-and-reuse/a-court-before-the-consequence/when-an-approval-reaches-the-effect/) can help participants maintain the account; it cannot appoint the people entitled to decide for them. Discovery and adoption ask whether a contribution is useful. Governance asks how the resulting relationships remain answerable to their participants.

[DAOnet](https://daostudio.in/daonet/) and the protocol proposals around it are a design for infrastructure for an economy of these capabilities: discovery and composition, memory management, observability, execution and transactions. A protocol earns its place by making a dependency easier to understand or an interaction easier to coordinate. Its value would have to reach the contributor and consumer doing useful work, rather than depend entirely on the network becoming large.

There is also a deeper, more speculative possibility: identifying learned capacities inside models and recombining them. That would require understanding their representations and dependencies, beyond the interfaces that connect separate capabilities. [Application composition](https://daostudio.in/collections/composing-ai-work/) offers a concrete starting point for reuse, with intermediate state, execution records and feedback that surrounding people and software can already reason about. The wider ambition asks how much more of useful intelligence could become an intelligible contribution.
