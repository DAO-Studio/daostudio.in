DAOnet

DAOnet is the DAO Studio protocol design for carrying a capability beyond the application that first made it, as YojnAI composes applications and YOJN Care coordinates response; no marketplace runs. It connects discovery, agreed use and shared execution with feedback that can improve what the next team receives.

Let useful intelligence accumulate

Share intelligences, the capabilities agents draw on, and playbooks with their versions and terms understood. Keep the context and results of their use available to the people improving them.

Questions about DAOnet

How does DAOnet differ from YojnAI?

YojnAI composes an application. DAOnet extends reuse across applications and teams, including the context, execution and transactions around each contribution.

What travels with a capability?

Its version, conditions of use and the context needed to interpret its results. The shared account connects those to who took part and what happened; people still judge whether the result is useful.

How can we obtain DAOnet?

RawThoughts consulting is the route for the reuse, shared execution or exchange problem you want it to address, with scope, implementation and applicable licensing agreed for the engagement.
