# DAOnet

DAOnet is the [DAO Studio](https://daostudio.in/) protocol design for carrying a capability beyond the application that first made it, as YojnAI composes applications and YOJN Care coordinates response; no marketplace runs. It connects discovery, agreed use and shared execution with feedback that can improve what the next team receives.

## Let useful intelligence accumulate

Share intelligences, the capabilities agents draw on, and playbooks with their versions and terms understood. Keep the context and results of their use available to the people improving them.

<a id="from-reuse-to-improvement"></a>

## From reuse to improvement

The design’s marketplace connects contributors, applications and human feedback.

### Contribute a capability

Offer an intelligence or playbook with its version and conditions of use.

### Use it in an application

Agents combine selected capabilities, keeping traces of what they used and what happened.

### Agree the terms

Participants retain control of access, consent and permitted use.

### Learn from its use

Human feedback informs revised intelligences and playbooks for the next application.

## Questions about DAOnet

**How does DAOnet differ from YojnAI?**

[YojnAI](https://daostudio.in/yojnai/) composes an application. DAOnet extends reuse across applications and teams, including the context, execution and transactions around each contribution.

**What travels with a capability?**

Its version, conditions of use and the context needed to interpret its results. The shared account connects those to who took part and what happened; people still judge whether the result is useful.
