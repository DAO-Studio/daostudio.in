Questions about the products

How can we discuss a product or purchase?

The products are paused. DAO Studio’s products remain available through RawThoughts consulting. Bring your requirements to discuss purchase, delivery scope and licensing with RTEPL.

How do the three products differ?

YOJN Care connects the people and evidence needed for a continuing response. YojnAI gives teams a composition they can inspect and improve. DAOnet carries reusable capability across applications. Each solves a different coordination problem; they need not be adopted as one platform.
