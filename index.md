## Questions about the products

**How can we discuss a product or purchase?**

The products are paused. DAO Studio’s products remain available through [RawThoughts consulting](https://rawthoughts.in/services/). Bring your requirements to discuss purchase, delivery scope and licensing with RTEPL.

**How do the three products differ?**

YOJN Care connects the people and evidence needed for a continuing response. YojnAI gives teams a composition they can inspect and improve. DAOnet carries reusable capability across applications. Each solves a different coordination problem; they need not be adopted as one platform.

**Where can I read more about the work?**

The [inquiries](https://daostudio.in/collections/) develop incident coordination, application composition and reuse. [About DAO Studio](https://daostudio.in/about/) connects those questions to the studio’s purpose, people and consulting route.
