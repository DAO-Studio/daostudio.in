# Follow an incident

YOJN Care is not a live emergency or dispatch service; this guide explains its response design. If you need help now, call your local emergency number.

Imagine a shop camera detecting a person after closing time. A second camera supplies another view. An operator calls the owner to ask whether the person is authorised staff, but the shift ends before the owner replies. The next operator needs both views, the question already asked and the work still waiting. Starting again from the latest alert would lose part of the incident.

[YOJN Care](https://daostudio.in/yojn-care/) brings those observations and actions together, connecting the person asking for help with their community, monitoring operators and authorised responders.

<a id="continue-the-incident-not-just-the-latest-alert"></a>

## Continue the incident, not just the latest alert

The operator compares the camera frames with the shop's reference view and instructions. A person in the image might be authorised staff; the pictures alone do not settle it. The operator records the call and the unanswered question. Later notifications add evidence to this incident instead of starting another investigation of the same event.

A neighbour may have opened the alert without agreeing to investigate. The incoming operator needs to know who has actually agreed to continue the review. Opening an alert, reviewing it and dispatching help therefore need separate states. The [monitoring guide](https://daostudio.in/yojn-care/review-and-handover/) follows this handover, and [Who carries the incident?](https://daostudio.in/collections/coordinating-emergencies/who-carries-the-incident/) explores the responsibility behind it.

<a id="someone-can-ask-for-help-directly"></a>

## Someone can ask for help directly

SOS begins with a person asking for help. It needs no camera image to make that request meaningful. High Alert raises a concern for assessment, as does an automatic detection; the reviewer needs to know which kind of request arrived.

Both personal actions are accessible with one hand. Groups, location sharing, camera sessions and incident evidence can give others context. After the gesture, the person needs to see whether the request is still sending, has reached the service or is being handled. [Understand a personal request](https://daostudio.in/yojn-care/personal-requests/) follows those states and what happens if the person corrects or cancels it.

<a id="give-the-reviewer-a-usable-view"></a>

## Give the reviewer a usable view

Monitoring a shop starts with its existing cameras, the hours when nobody should be there and the owner's instructions. A computer at the premises, or compatible camera software, supplies detections and media for review. The installation, connection and cameras need continuing maintenance; an old image cannot answer whether somebody is still inside.

The operator also needs the earlier handling. An old image and an unanswered call leave different questions open: one needs a current view, the other an answer from the owner. [The evidence path](https://daostudio.in/collections/coordinating-emergencies/the-evidence-path/) follows those inputs from the premises to the reviewer.

<a id="keep-someone-available-to-continue"></a>

## Keep someone available to continue

Sharing monitoring across premises and involving the community can make coverage more affordable. Grouping related observations also avoids asking several people to investigate the same thing from scratch. But somebody still needs to be available when a case arrives, and an [unanswered case](https://daostudio.in/collections/coordinating-emergencies/the-cases-that-are-still-waiting/) must remain visible while they work on it.

A response service needs agreed staffing, coverage, maintenance and authorised response arrangements. Emergency-service integration requires those services to participate; it cannot be supplied by a button in the application. [Staffing and maintenance](https://daostudio.in/collections/coordinating-emergencies/the-cost-of-continuing-response/) explains what continuing that coverage involves.
