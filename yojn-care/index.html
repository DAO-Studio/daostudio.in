YOJN Care

YOJN Care is the DAO Studio product for emergency response, beside YojnAI and DAOnet. It connects personal requests, existing cameras and human response for homes, shops and neighbourhoods. The product is paused, and YOJN Care is not a live emergency or dispatch service.

If you need help now, call your local emergency number.

Questions about YOJN Care

Who is YOJN Care for?

Homes, shops and neighbourhoods, and the monitoring teams who review their incidents.

Does an alert mean help has been dispatched?

No. Raising an alert, reviewing it, dispatching help and arriving are separate steps. Follow an incident explains the response design.

How can we obtain YOJN Care?

YOJN Care remains available through RawThoughts consulting. Bring the response workflow you need. Scope, delivery and licensing are agreed for the engagement.
