# YOJN Care

YOJN Care is the [DAO Studio](https://daostudio.in/) product for emergency response, beside [YojnAI](https://daostudio.in/yojnai/) and [DAOnet](https://daostudio.in/daonet/). It connects personal requests, existing cameras and human response for homes, shops and neighbourhoods. The product is paused, and YOJN Care is not a live emergency or dispatch service.

If you need help now, call your local emergency number.

## Keep the incident together

### Personal SOS

A person asks for help.

### High Alert

A person raises a concern.

### Camera observation

A camera notices someone after hours.

### Human review

Check the evidence, site instructions and what people report.

### Response handover

Carry the evidence and open questions to someone who agrees to continue.

A shop's camera views and an unanswered owner call stay together when another operator takes over. [Follow the incident](https://daostudio.in/yojn-care/review-and-handover/).

## Questions about YOJN Care

**Who is YOJN Care for?**

Homes, shops and neighbourhoods, and the monitoring teams who review their incidents.

**Does an alert mean help has been dispatched?**

No. Raising an alert, reviewing it, dispatching help and arriving are separate steps. [Follow an incident](https://daostudio.in/yojn-care/follow-an-incident/) explains the response design.

**How can we obtain YOJN Care?**

YOJN Care remains available through [RawThoughts consulting](https://rawthoughts.in/services/) (on RawThoughts). Bring the response workflow you need. Scope, delivery and licensing are agreed for the engagement.
