YojnAI

YojnAI is the DAO Studio product for composing AI applications, between YOJN Care’s response work and DAOnet’s reuse across teams. It works between the model call and the final answer, moving beyond “0 or 1”: inspect an agent’s result, supply the context it lacks and continue from there. YojnAI is paused as a product.

Questions about YojnAI

Who is YojnAI for?

Teams building AI applications and the people who know the operation those applications serve. Low-code composition lets builders reuse work; traces and feedback let operators help improve it.

Does feedback change the application immediately?

No. The team evaluates a candidate improvement and chooses which version to use. Review a disputed answer follows that choice.

Where has YojnAI been used?

Pilots in investment banking, operations and the CFO office covered invoices, agreements and research calculations. The partner companies own those applications. The deployment account explains the data paths and reported model results with their limits.

How can we obtain YojnAI?

YojnAI remains available through RawThoughts consulting. Each engagement sets the application scope, integration, deployment and licensing for your use.
